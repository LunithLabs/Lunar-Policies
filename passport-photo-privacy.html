Privacy Policy — Passport & ID Photo Maker

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Your photo never leaves your computer. Face detection and background removal run entirely inside your own browser. There is no server of ours, no account to create, and no tracking of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server, no database and no analytics. We do not collect your name, your email address, your location, your browsing history, or anything about how you use the extension.

Your photo

Every photo you open with this extension is read into your browser's memory, processed there, and saved back to your own computer as a download. Your photos are never uploaded, never transmitted, and never seen by us or anyone else.

This is a property of how the extension is built, not a promise about how we behave: the extension has no permission to send your photos anywhere. It can reach exactly one internet address, described below.

The one network request

If — and only if — you buy a licence and click Activate, the extension sends the licence key you typed to our payment provider, Gumroad, so it can confirm the purchase is genuine:

POST https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify

That request contains your licence key and the product identifier. It contains nothing else — not your files, not your name, not your email. As with any request to any website, Gumroad will also see your IP address; their handling of it is governed by Gumroad's own privacy policy.

If you never enter a licence key, the extension never makes a network request at all and works entirely offline.

What is stored on your computer

The extension stores a small amount of data in your browser's local extension storage, on your machine only:

  • your licence key, if you have activated one;
  • the date you activated it;
  • a count of how many tasks you have run today, used for the free daily allowance.

This never leaves your computer, is not synced, and is not readable by us or by any website. Removing the extension deletes it.

Permissions, and why they exist

  • storage — to keep the three items listed above.
  • Access to api.gumroad.com — solely for the licence check described above. This is the only website the extension is permitted to contact.

The extension requests no permission to read web pages, no access to your browsing history, no access to the contents of your tabs, and no access to any other website.

The AI models

This extension includes two machine-learning models: one that locates facial landmarks so your head can be positioned to the published size rules, and one that separates you from your background so the required plain background can be applied.

Both models are packaged inside the extension and run on your own device. Your photograph is never sent anywhere. No image, and no biometric measurement derived from it, is transmitted, stored by us, or retained after you close the tab. Nothing is downloaded at run time — the extension works with no internet connection at all.

Children

Passport photos are often taken for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all — a child's photo is processed on your device exactly like an adult's and is never transmitted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Because the extension collects nothing, any change here would follow a change in the extension itself, which would be described in its Chrome Web Store listing.
